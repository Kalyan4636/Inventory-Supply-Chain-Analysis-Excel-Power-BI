# 📊 Inventory & Supply Chain Analysis | Excel & Power BI

> **An end-to-end data analytics project focused on inventory performance, warehouse utilization, transportation costs, sales trends, lead times, and backorder management.**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **inventory and supply chain operations** to understand operational performance, identify potential inefficiencies, and generate actionable business insights.

The analysis was performed using **Microsoft Excel** for data cleaning, preparation, and exploratory analysis, followed by **Power BI** for data modeling, KPI development, interactive visualization, and dashboard reporting.

The project demonstrates how operational data can be transformed into meaningful insights to support **inventory optimization, supply chain monitoring, warehouse management, and operational decision-making**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The key objectives of this analysis were to:

* Analyze overall inventory performance
* Measure warehouse utilization
* Calculate inventory-related KPIs
* Analyze inventory turnover and Days Sales of Inventory
* Identify transportation cost patterns
* Compare sales performance across years
* Analyze units sold by category and region
* Evaluate average lead time across product categories
* Monitor backorder fulfillment status
* Analyze inventory distribution across warehouses, regions, and categories
* Build an interactive management dashboard

2. **Days Sales of Inventory** stands at **15.56 days**, indicating the inventory coverage represented by the project's calculation methodology.

3. **Inventory turnover ratio** is **23.47**, representing the calculated inventory turnover during the analyzed period.

7. **Inventory levels** vary across categories and regions, enabling deeper analysis of inventory allocation and operational performance.

## 📐 KPI Definitions

### 🏭 Warehouse Utilization

Measures the proportion of available warehouse capacity being utilized.

### 📦 Days Sales of Inventory (DSI)

Measures approximately how many days of inventory are represented based on inventory and sales/COGS inputs.

### 🔄 Inventory Turnover Ratio

Measures how frequently inventory is turned over during the selected period.

### 🔁 Backorder Fulfillment

Tracks backorders according to their fulfillment status:

* Fulfilled
* Pending
* Cancelled
